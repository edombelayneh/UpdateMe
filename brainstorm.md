# 🧠 Brainstorm – Capstone App: UpdateMe

## 💡 Problem Statement
I come from a culture where it's considered rude to be on your phone in front of others while they’re talking to you. I’ve grown up being present and respectful in social settings. Because of that, I tend to stay off my phone when I’m with people—even if they’re on theirs.

This summer, I’ll be interning in California, and my boyfriend will be in Texas. We’re both busy and in different time zones, but he really values consistent check-ins and updates. I don’t want to seem distant, but I also don’t want to be rude in social settings. I need a way to keep him in the loop **without constantly texting**.

## 🎯 App Purpose
The app, *UpdateMe*, lets me send quick updates to my partner throughout the day—without needing to type out long messages or stay glued to my phone. It automates scheduled check-ins, offers one-tap status buttons, and helps maintain emotional closeness even when we’re far apart.

## 👥 Target Users
- People in long-distance relationships
- People with busy schedules who want to stay connected
- Anyone wanting to send quick life updates to a loved one without constant texting

## 🔧 Core Features (MVP)
- Pre-scheduled daily updates (morning, night, etc.)
- Quick-tap status updates (like “At work” or “With friends”)
- Partner profile with latest updates

## 🌟 Stretch Features
- Mood slider or emoji reactions
- Audio messages/voice notes
- Shared countdown widget (e.g., “10 days until we see each other!”)
- Shared digital journal or message board
- Outfit suggestion assistant (inspired by "Fashion AI")

## 💬 Notes
- Built using UIKit and Storyboard
- Design will be soft, cute, minimal — official icon is a bell
